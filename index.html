About

PhotoPedagogy Remix is directly inspired by Alan Huck and Jonas Feige’s The Mute Testimony. What happens when pictures meet words — especially when the pictures are drawn from the histories of photography and the words belong to a multitude of commentators on the medium?

A random association of words and images generates productive tension, engaging the mind’s pattern-making capabilities. How does this relate to that?

“Photographs rely on chance, more or less.”— Threshold Concept #6, PhotoPedagogy

As the name suggests, PhotoPedagogy Remix takes selected quotations from photopedagogy.com and pairs them with photographic images also referenced there. Over the years, the site has grown into a substantial resource, free to teachers and students everywhere. This app is a compressed, randomised version of the image / text combinations on the website.

We have reproduced images for which we don’t own the rights in the hope that visitors have access to a wide range of imagery, and that the various rights holders grant us permission to share these images freely — for educational purposes and not for profit. If you own the rights to any image shown here and would like it removed, please get in touch.

Remix to your heart’s content.
